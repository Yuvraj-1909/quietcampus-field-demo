Learn & settings

Understand the numbers

Clear context for every sound observation.

What is dB(A)?

A-weighting adjusts a sound measurement to reflect how human hearing responds across frequencies. A phone reading depends on its microphone and audio processing.

What is LAeq?

LAeq is the energy-average sound level over a stated measurement period. Sound levels must be averaged in energy space, not by taking a simple arithmetic mean of decibels.

Small ways to reduce exposure

When practical, step farther from a loud source, choose a quieter route or place, and lower personal audio volume. These are general awareness tips, not a personal medical assessment.

Calibration designation

For this project, the imported NoiseCapture readings are designated calibrated by the project owner. The archive metadata itself lists gain calibration 0.00 and method “None”; that original source information is preserved with each session.

What this does not say

A short campus observation does not establish a student’s full-day exposure or diagnose hearing damage. No hearing-damage percentage or medical risk score is calculated.

Privacy

Measurement capture is not connected. The prototype does not request microphone permission, save audio, transcribe speech, or share location automatically.

Location context

The phone was placed around the center of the noisy area. GPS samples show where the surveys were recorded; they remain historical observations, not real-time campus levels.
